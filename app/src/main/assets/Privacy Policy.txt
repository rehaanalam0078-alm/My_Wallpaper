# Privacy Policy for My Wallpaper Studio

**Effective Date:** September 12, 2026

**Last Updated:** September 12, 2026

Md Rehaan Alam ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **My Wallpaper Studio** mobile application ("Application").

This Privacy Policy explains how My Wallpaper Studio collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Application.

By using My Wallpaper Studio, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Application, we may collect or process the following information.

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number, where provided during account registration
* Firebase user ID (UID)
* Profile information and profile photo associated with your authenticated account

You may create an account using email/password authentication or Google Sign-In.

This information is used to authenticate users, maintain accounts, provide personalized features, and associate user-generated content with the correct account.

### 1.2 Wallpaper and App Activity

The Application may process information associated with your use of wallpaper features, including:

* Wallpapers you favorite
* Wallpapers you download
* AI wallpaper creations
* AI generation prompts
* Enhanced or processed prompts associated with AI generation
* Wallpaper and category information required to provide Application functionality

Some favorite information is stored locally on your device using Android application storage.

### 1.3 AI-Generated Content

My Wallpaper Studio provides AI-powered wallpaper-generation functionality.

When you use AI generation, information such as your text prompt may be processed by third-party AI services or infrastructure used to generate the requested image.

Generated images and related information may be stored in services used by the Application so that you can access your creations through your account.

You should not submit confidential, highly sensitive, or personal information in AI prompts unless necessary for the requested functionality.

### 1.4 Device and Technical Information

The Application and third-party SDKs used by the Application may automatically process certain technical information necessary for operation, security, diagnostics, and service improvement.

This may include information such as:

* Device and operating-system information
* Application version
* Basic technical identifiers
* Diagnostic information
* Usage and event information
* Information required by third-party SDKs to provide their services

We do not intentionally request your precise physical location for the core wallpaper functionality of the Application.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed through My Wallpaper Studio may be used to:

* Create and maintain your account
* Authenticate your identity

* Display and manage wallpapers
* Save and synchronize account-related information
* Maintain your favorite wallpapers

* Generate AI wallpapers

* Maintain application security and reliability
* Diagnose technical problems and crashes
* Understand application usage and improve features
* Provide notifications and other Application functionality where enabled

* Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

My Wallpaper Studio relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

### 3.1 Firebase

We use services provided by **Google Firebase**, including services associated with authentication, databases, configuration, analytics, and application infrastructure.

Firebase may process information such as account identifiers, authentication information, application usage information, and technical/diagnostic information depending on the Firebase services used by the Application.

Firebase services may include:

* Firebase Authentication
* Firebase Firestore
* Firebase Realtime Database
* Firebase Analytics
* Firebase Remote Config
* Firebase and Google cloud infrastructure supporting application functionality

Google's handling of information through its services is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies.

### 3.2 Google Sign-In

When you choose Google Sign-In, authentication is provided through Google.

Information made available to the Application through the sign-in process may include information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address, profile information, and an authentication identifier, subject to the permissions and configuration of the sign-in service.

### 3.3 AI Services

AI wallpaper generation may use third-party AI services and Google/third-party infrastructure.

Your AI prompt and information necessary to generate an image may be transmitted to the applicable AI service.

The processing of such information is subject to the applicable third-party provider's terms and privacy practices.

### 3.4 Other Third-Party Libraries and Services

The Application may contain third-party libraries or software development kits required for features such as image loading, analytics, cloud services, storage, authentication, diagnostics, or AI functionality.

Such services may process limited technical or application information as necessary to provide their functionality.

We are responsible for ensuring that our disclosures remain consistent with the actual services incorporated into the Application.

## 4. Notifications

My Wallpaper Studio may provide application notifications related to application functionality, such as newly available wallpaper content or other relevant updates.

Where Android requires notification permission, notifications are subject to the permission granted by you.

You may control notification access through the Application, where such controls are provided, or through your Android device settings.

For example, wallpapers may be downloaded to a Pictures/MyWallpaper location on your device.

We do not use access to your device's media for purposes unrelated to the Application's functionality.
